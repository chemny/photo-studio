- Prefer one preview generation, with at most one targeted retry for a clear defect; preserve approved content during revisions.
- Align optional composition helpers with the current ratios, support six-panel layouts and landscape nine-panel family sheets, and verify pixel preservation.

Image generation requires an image tool supplied by the host Agent. Visual identity and body checks require image review; script checks alone do not certify resemblance or official acceptance.
